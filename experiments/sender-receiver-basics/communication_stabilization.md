Communication Stabilization

Objective

To observe how communication between agents becomes stable through repeated interaction and rewards.

---

What the Experiment Does

- Sender sends messages based on current policy.
- Receiver interprets the message and acts.
- Correct coordination gives positive reward.
- Failed communication changes the message policy.

Over multiple episodes:

- useful communication patterns stabilize.

---

Observations

- Initial communication may be inconsistent.
- Failed mappings gradually disappear.
- Successful mappings survive longer.
- Coordination accuracy improves over time.

---

Main Understanding

Useful communication becomes stable because reward reinforcement preserves successful message patterns.
