Noisy Communication Channel

Objective

To understand how communication reliability affects coordination performance in multi-agent systems.

---

What the Experiment Does

- Sender sends a message.
- Noise may randomly alter the message.
- Receiver acts using the received message.
- Rewards depend on communication success.

---

Observations

- Noise causes communication failures.
- Incorrect messages reduce coordination accuracy.
- Higher noise leads to unstable coordination.
- Reliable communication improves task performance.

---

Main Understanding

Communication systems must remain reliable and resistant to noise for successful multi-agent coordination.
