PettingZoo RL Training

Objective

To understand real MARL environment interaction using PettingZoo.

---

What the Experiment Does

- Multiple agents interact inside a TicTacToe environment.
- Agents receive:
  - observations,
  - rewards,
  - valid actions.
- Environment cycles through agents sequentially.

The experiment demonstrates:

- real MARL workflow,
- environment stepping,
- and multi-agent interaction.

---

Observations

- Multiple agents share the same environment.
- Environment controls turn order.
- Rewards change dynamically during gameplay.
- Valid action masks restrict illegal moves.

---

Main Understanding

Real MARL environments contain multiple interacting agents with decentralized observations and shared environment dynamics.
